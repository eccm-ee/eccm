# State of radio

## The concept

The team's concept, "tulevik, mida ei tulnud" (16 August 2026), describes two branches of one rhizome: not a platform and its app, not a centre and its periphery, but two plateaus, connected and equal.

**Raadio Tallinn 1965** is the web platform: local, curated, and anchored in one idea, the electronic music studio and radio that could have existed in Tallinn in 1965 beside Paris, Cologne and Milan. Not nostalgia for something that existed, but the path history did not take, and the music that could have come from it.

**Väin**, or Strait, is the open app, lendable to any initiative, archive or community that shares the principles. It is not Raadio Tallinn 1965 in another medium. The name comes from Deleuze's minor literature: the listener is a strait, a narrow place through which something larger and unrepresented briefly passes.

Both share four principles:

- **Room for voices outside the big register.** Amateurs, accents, dialects, demos, unfinished work.
- **Flow, not archive.** Much of the programme cannot be heard again. Some shows air once, some repeat deliberately shifted.
- **Collective authorship.** No voice speaks for an "us" that already exists.
- **An open identity.** "A tendency rather than a brand."

## Where reality differs

The two names are not the mess. The concept gives each a clear job, and what exists does the opposite.

- **Raadio Tallinn 1965 has no web platform.** It has a Linktree, a Substack, Instagram and Vimeo, so the Linktree is the front door.
- **Väin acts as Raadio Tallinn 1965's app.** Its About screen says "an app created for the 'Radio Tallinn 1965' project" and "Be connected with ECCM". Its always-on channel reads "Radio 1965 on radio1965". Nobody else has borrowed it.
- **Väin archives everything.** Broadcasts are recorded by default, copied to `eccm.ee`, and shelved a day later.
- **Väin cannot be lent.** Its code has no licence, and the app says "© Tarmo Johannes".
- **The names are spelled five ways.** Radio Tallinn 1965, Raadio Tallinn 1965, Radio 1965, VÄIN or Väin, Tallinn 1965 or Studio Tallinn 1965. A look can vary, but a name spelled five ways cannot be searched for.
- **It is ECCM's, and the public side does not say so.** The app, the privacy policy, the recordings and the studio are ECCM's. Linktree, Instagram and Substack never mention it.

## Who it is for

- **Listeners** want to hear it and know what is on, in one tap from a QR code. Today it takes TestFlight and a beta app, on an iPhone.
- **Contributors** want to broadcast and belong. Väin does that well, and guests can send from their own software.
- **Other communities** that might borrow Väin need a way in, a licence and a space of their own. None exists yet.
- **Readers and funders** want the idea, and the concept text serves them. Listeners need one line each, such as "Raadio Tallinn 1965: the studio Tallinn never got" and "Väin: a strait anyone can broadcast through".
- **Guests from Germany** and their listeners need English.

## What works

- **The idea.** A radio from a past that never came, with real depth behind it.
- **Two names with two jobs,** once the jobs are visible.
- **The Instagram visuals.**
- **Väin for broadcasters.** Press a button at a bar or in the studio and you are on air.
- **The speed.** An app, a server and live broadcasts between July and October, by one person.

## What does not

- **No way in.** A newcomer lands on a Linktree, which reads as a placeholder.
- **Listening is too hard.** Only a beta app on an iPhone, and the plain stream will not play on an https page.
- **It looks unfinished.** The half-written post, "Deleteme" in the feed, 7 followers. Rough edges read as intended only when chosen.

## Where the concept needs an answer

- **Flow against documentation.** The concept wants much of the programme gone after it airs. Üle Heli, ECCM and funders will want recordings. Väin records everything by default. So each show is marked **kept**, recorded and playable afterwards, or **once**, live only, and the page says which.
- **An open identity against being findable.** The look can vary. The names must not.
- **Lendable needs groundwork.** An open licence, a server that is not a volunteer's box, someone who moderates, and someone who pays.
- **ECCM's place.** The concept does not name ECCM, the app thanks it, the public side hides it. Say once how they relate.

## Next steps

The festival comes first because it has a date. The rest can go in any order.

### Make the festival work

Üle Heli, 23 to 25 October. Aivar Tõnso's *Päevamenüü* runs at ECCM Friday to Sunday, 12 to 18 or 14 to 16: radio experiments, talks and workshops, Thomas Ankersmit among the guests. It streams on `radio1965`, with channels for Üle Heli and the German guests.

- **Channels.** A mount `yleheli` and one per German guest, each with its own password and name, so cards read "Üle Heli on yleheli", not "Kalle on user2". See [channels from a file](/docs/vain-tech-notes#channels-from-a-file-on-the-server).
- **Listening.** An https address per channel ([https for audio](/docs/vain-tech-notes#https-for-audio)), a web player on `ule-heli.ee`, which Tarmo offered to make, and a QR code at ECCM leading to it.
- **Pushes and access.** No push to every app user from each guest ([one push topic](/docs/vain-tech-notes#one-push-topic-for-everyone)), and the server checking who may publish ([who may write](/docs/vain-tech-notes#who-may-write)).
- **Kept or once,** decided per show ([defaults per channel](/docs/vain-tech-notes#defaults-per-channel-in-the-hook)), and a short feedback form.
- **Tidy first.** Finish the Substack post, clear the test items from the feed, settle the spellings.

Nothing new to maintain, but afterwards the player belongs to Üle Heli and Raadio Tallinn 1965 still has no platform.

### Väin as an open space

```text
väin.ee
```

Free in the registry on 9 October. `vain.ee` is taken.

A space any community can borrow. The real test of the concept is a second community using it.

- **An open licence** on Väin's code, so it can be lent and outlive any one person.
- **A space per community,** with its own channels, cards and contributors.
- **A server of its own.** Väin runs on `live.uuu.ee`, a server the ensemble U: lends. A space others borrow needs a server someone is committed to keeping up.
- **Its own privacy policy.** Today's names ECCM as responsible for users' data. Open to other communities, it has to name whoever runs Väin.
- **One sentence on ECCM.** Whether Väin is ECCM's project, supported by ECCM, or independent, said once on its site.

### Experiment: Väin on the web

An alternative to try beside the native app, not instead of it: Väin as a web app, from a link, with no store and no review. eccm and positron already run every part:

- **Installable:** a manifest and a service worker (`public/manifest.webmanifest`, `public/sw.js`).
- **Push notifications:** Web Push with the site's own key, no Firebase, on Android, desktop, and iPhone from the home screen (`src/push.mjs`).
- **Playing with the phone locked:** positron's HLS station, kept under the tag `radio-last` (`archive/radio.md`).
- **Recording when asked,** into Cloudflare's storage.
- **Cards and shelves** from Väin's feed, which is JSON already.

Against it: a browser stops the microphone when the screen locks, so broadcasting on the move stays native. iPhone pushes need the page added to the home screen first. And it is a second codebase beside Tarmo's. Worth a small test with one community before anyone commits to it.

### Raadio Tallinn 1965's web platform

```text
raadiotallinn1965.ee
raadiotallinn1965.väin.ee
väin.ee/raadiotallinn1965
```

What the concept says Raadio Tallinn 1965 is. One address of three, chosen before going live, since moving it breaks saved links and posters:

- **Its own domain,** free on 9 October. Stands alone, a second registration.
- **A subdomain of Väin,** the first space hosted there. Still its own app and notifications to a browser, but reads as "on Väin".
- **A path on Väin.** Simplest, but every community then shares one app, one notification permission and one set of logins.

On the page:

- **Live now:** every channel on air, with a play button.
- **Today:** the programme.
- **Kept:** only the shows marked kept. A once-only show says so.
- **An address per channel,** like `/yleheli`, for guests to share.
- Estonian and English.
- Väin linked as the way to broadcast.

It replaces the Linktree, and needs no editor: it reads Väin's feed and the live server. It needs the https stream, a domain, and someone who keeps the programme current. It can run on the Cloudflare account `eccm.ee` uses, at no cost.
