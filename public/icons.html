Admin/Research/Icons

Icons

Play, pause and stop from seven open icon sets, each at 16, 20, 24 and 32 px in the ink, then inside a .btn-sm and a .btn, alone and beside the word, on the light ground and the dark. The stroked sets are drawn with a stroke of 1 and vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke, so their line is one CSS pixel at every size, the weight of the site’s rules and of the mark’s stave; the filled sets cannot be, and their line grows and shrinks with the size.

Recommended: Feather. Its play is a plain triangle and its pause two plain bars with no radius drawn into them, so with mitred joins it is the only set as square as the .btn around it, and at a stroke of 1 its line measures 1 CSS px at 16, 20, 24 and 32, where Phosphor thin measures 0.5 px at 16 and Carbon 1.5 px at 24.

The play button

.play-btn on a .btn-sm or a .btn: a square as tall as the button, its 1px edge and inward hover, the Feather glyph at half the square. data-state names the glyph, play, pause or stop; the label is a visually hidden word beside it; aria-pressed="true" fills it, as /radio fills its Stop. Not yet on /radio, /admin/files or the recordings.

Light: play, pause, stop, then pressed, then disabled

Dark: play, pause, stop, then pressed, then disabled

As /radio would use it, one per channel; press one to see it change
