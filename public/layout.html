The page

One header and one footer over six event pages, each in the scheme the derive page read off its picture: the header with the mark and its wordmark on one line, centred, the menu at 16 px; the footer in three columns with the City of Music mark at the end. At the desk each page spans the window; beside it the same page in a 375 px box, where the menu sits behind five hairlines on the stave's own rows.

Estonian Centre of Contemporary Music, Pühavaimu 9, Tallinn
