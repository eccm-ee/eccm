Sans alternatives

Six sans faces in the derived page's three uses: the default, Instrument Sans, and the four fresher faces tried on 2026-09-27, each at its normal width. Each is shown the way a derived page uses it: the title, the facts and the credits in the face over the site's sans; the body in the face too, which the page allows on a ground near white; and a dark derived ground where the body stays in the sans.

A. Schibsted Grotesk, the default. The site's own face in every role, which is what a page whose picture reads as sans gets; the three blocks below are one face throughout.

The derived page: title, facts and credits in the face, the body in Schibsted Grotesk
