Serif alternatives

Three faces kept from a page of thirteen: Schibsted Grotesk, the site's own, Instrument Sans, and Literata, the one serif left of ten tried in place of Source Serif 4. Each is shown the way a derived page uses it: the title, the facts and the credits in the face over the site's sans; the body in the face too, which the page allows on a ground near white; and a dark derived ground where the body stays in the sans.

K. Schibsted Grotesk, the default. The site's own face in every role, which is what a page whose picture reads as sans gets; the three blocks below are one face throughout.

The derived page: title, facts and credits in the serif, the body in Schibsted Grotesk
