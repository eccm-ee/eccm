Serif alternatives

Ten modern serifs in place of Source Serif 4, the face a page takes when its picture reads as serif. Each is shown the way that page uses it: the title, the facts and the credits in the serif over the site's sans; the body in the serif too, which the page allows on a ground near white; and a dark derived ground where the body stays in the sans. No Garamond and no other old-style revival.

A. Fraunces, weights 100 to 900, optical sizes 9 to 144. A soft, old-style-flavoured serif of 2020 with an optical size axis that goes wonky at display sizes and quiet at text sizes; the most character of the ten.

The derived page: title, facts and credits in the serif, the body in Schibsted Grotesk
