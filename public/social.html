Events

Each event: its share card, and on the line under it its Instagram cards at 1080 by 1350, Instagram's 4:5, the text card and the picture split across two cards, so a carousel's swipe shows it whole, the text card's type larger since a feed is read on a phone. The share card is the image an event page names as its og:image, 1200 by 630 PNG, rendered by the Worker from the event's own rows: the title in the face its picture's scheme reads, Roboto Serif or Instrument Sans, then the date, time and venue in the second ink, and the mark at the foot with its own short stave, all on the scheme's ground.
