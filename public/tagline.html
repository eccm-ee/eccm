Tagline in DIN

The line under the mark in DIN Condensed, the face nearest the original, in every combination of casing, tracking and wrap, fitted to the letters and in ink, a shorter line centred on the letters or starting on the E: 48 settings (the greys and the line wider than the letters were asked off on 2026-09-27). Each is drawn at the phone's 11rem in English and in Estonian, with the size in the logo's units and the x-height on a phone under it. Nothing is squeezed: a fitted line is sized to the letters' width, E to M.
