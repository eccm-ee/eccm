Type specimens

One event row in each candidate face, at the live page's sizes. The site sets its body in Public Sans and its headings in Instrument Sans; the rest are the alternatives looked at.

Look at the title beside the mark, the 14 px line, the paragraph at 66 characters, and Š, Ž, Õ, Ä, Ö, Ü.

A. Schibsted Grotesk, one variable file per subset. A newspaper's grotesque, 2023, made for headlines and columns; more character than Inter, less than Archivo. CHOSEN 2026-09-26, the live page since.

Kõikides aktsioonides on osalistele toeks dramaturg Alissa Šnaider.

B. Source Serif 4 for everything. A text serif with an optical size axis; the calmest long read on white, and the furthest from the mark.

C. Instrument Sans. A text grotesk of 2022 with a designed width axis; the body at 100, titles at 85, so the headings lean toward the mark without a squeeze. The cleanest lowercase of the five.
