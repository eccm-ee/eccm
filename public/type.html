In the derived page's three uses

Each face the way a derived page uses it: the title, the facts and the credits in the face over the site's sans; the body in the face too, which the page allows on a ground near white; and a dark derived ground where the body stays in the sans. The six sans first, then the ten serifs.

A. Schibsted Grotesk, the default. The site's own face in every role, which is what a page whose picture reads as sans gets; the three blocks below are one face throughout.
