Wordmark in Instrument Sans

Instrument Sans medium at width 75, lowercase, two lines a language, starting on the E, both languages at one size. Under the mark at 40 units, one language, or one language over the mark and the other under it, either way round; beside it to the right of the stave at 37 units, one language or both side by side, every block centred on the E's middle bar. The Estonian breaks inside its long word, the hyphen a hairline of the mark's weight. Each lockup at 20rem and 11rem of mark width, plain, in a frame with a one pixel border, and white on black, a square for the under layout and a rectangle for the beside one, 28 units from the ink. In a square with a language over the mark and one under it, the two sit at the margin and the mark stays centred. The page ends with the same under lockup in the four other sans faces of 2026-09-27, Bricolage Grotesque at width 87.5, Sofia Sans Condensed, Familjen Grotesk and Anybody at width 75, all medium, to compare against Instrument Sans.

Estonian Centre of Contemporary Music, Pühavaimu 9, Tallinn
