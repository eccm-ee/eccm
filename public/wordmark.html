Wordmark in Instrument Sans

Instrument Sans medium at width 75 on two lines fitted to the letters, the setting picked from the comparison, in four casings at the header's 20rem and the phone's 11rem, English and Estonian. The Estonian breaks inside its long word, the hyphen a hairline of the mark's weight. Both lines start on the E. The uppercase carries 0.06em; the rest no tracking.

Estonian Centre of Contemporary Music, Pühavaimu 9, Tallinn
