Wordmark in Instrument Sans

Instrument Sans medium at width 75, lowercase, two lines starting on the E. Three studies: the line height of the two lines under the mark; the size at the fit and three steps under it; and a layout beside the mark, to the right of the stave, in four shapes: two lines or one, centred on the E's middle bar or set on the letters' baseline. The Estonian breaks inside its long word, the hyphen a hairline of the mark's weight. English and Estonian at 20rem and 11rem of mark width.

Estonian Centre of Contemporary Music, Pühavaimu 9, Tallinn
