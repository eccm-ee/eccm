Wordmark in Instrument Sans

Instrument Sans medium at width 75, lowercase, two lines a language, starting on the E, both languages at one size. Under the mark at 40 units, one language, or one language over the mark and the other under it, either way round; beside it to the right of the stave at 37 units, one language or both side by side, every block centred on the E's middle bar. The Estonian breaks inside its long word, the hyphen a hairline of the mark's weight. Each lockup at 20rem and 11rem of mark width.

Estonian Centre of Contemporary Music, Pühavaimu 9, Tallinn
